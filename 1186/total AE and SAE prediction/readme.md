This folder contains files(pdfs and ipynbs) needed to predict total no. of AE and SAE for 1186 set. 

final_AE_SAE_prediction model uses previous month's actual total AEs/SAEs to predict next months total no. of AEs/SAEs , where as final_2_AE_SAE_prediction utilize the model utilises the predicted value of previous month to predict next months AE/SAE.
